# S3: AI in Cisco Certifications: Elevate Your Expertise [BRKCRT-1012]

## Session Description

Uncover the essential differences between "AI in the network" and "AI on the network," and understand how these concepts are reshaping the technological landscape. Join us for an insightful session that delves into the world of AI, exploring their transformative impact across various technologies and job roles and ever-growing demand for reskilling and upskilling.

This session will provide a deep dive into how Cisco is integrating AI skills across certification programs, including CCNA, CCNP, CCIE, and CCDE. We will discuss the inclusion of AI topics in these exams, ensuring that IT professionals are equipped with the essential knowledge and skills to harness AI technologies effectively in their roles.

Whether you're preparing for certification, or seeking to deepen your understanding of AI's role across technologies, this session offers valuable insights into the future of AI integration. Take advantage of this opportunity to stay at the forefront of technological advancements.
