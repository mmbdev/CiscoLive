# NTS SIEM in a Box [PARSEC-1336]

NOTE: Registering for this session does not guarantee you a seat. Seating is available on a first come, first seated basis.

Discover how NTS tackles highly individualized cybersecurity challenges with the NTS Threat Detection Service | SIEM - a truly comprehensive, 24×7, worry-free SOC service. Built on the NTS Defense Platform and powered by the leading technologies and AI capabilities of Splunk and Cisco UCS, the solution delivers powerful, scalable, and intelligent real-time security monitoring out of the box.

Behind the service stands NTS's dedicated team of highly trained Security Analysts, combining deep expertise with advanced AI and automation to ensure efficient investigation, response, and seamless scalability. The platform also provides full transparency across data, processes, and detections, giving organizations complete insight into their environment and every action taken. Its open and extensible architecture goes beyond security, enabling the integration of additional use cases such as observability, ITSM, and more, developed by your own teams or co-delivered with NTS. This flexibility allows organizations to evolve their operations ecosystem at their own pace while benefiting from NTS' proven expertise.
