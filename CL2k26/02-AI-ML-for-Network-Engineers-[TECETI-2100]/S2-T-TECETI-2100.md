# S2: AI-ML for Network Engineers: A Deep Dive [TECETI-2100]

## Session Description

Have you wanted to dive deeper into how AI/ML can benefit network engineers, and how you can begin to harness some of these powerful AI capabilities to address the issues and workflows within your own organization? If so, this is the session for you.  First, we’ll cover the evolution and theory of AI/ML algorithms, including neural networks and generative AI. Then we’ll take a deep dive into the hardware architectures that power these algorithms, from the ASICs and GPUs, to compute platforms, to AI-optimized data centers. We’ll also explore Cisco networking, security and collaboration products that leverage these features and demonstrate some of the powerful use-cases these enable for our customers. Additionally, we’ll also explore how Cisco is applying AI/ML thought-leadership to services and support.  Finally, we’ll explore future directions and innovations in AI, with a focus on AI agents and agentic architectures. Our Cisco Live Distinguished Speakers will take you on a journey through how AI/ML capabilities can be used to address some of the key issues faced by every network engineer and explore some of the toolsets and capabilities that every network engineer needs to become familiar with. Come to this fun and interactive session to discover exciting new capabilities that AI/ML has to offer for you and your organization.
