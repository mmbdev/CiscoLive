## Session Description

Are you ready to take a deep dive into Cisco Catalyst 9000 switches? This session is designed for networking professionals who want a clear, practical understanding of how these devices work.

We’ll cover the architecture and design of Catalyst 9000 switches for modern networks, focusing on both Silicon One and UADP chipsets. You’ll get a step-by-step walkthrough of how packets move through the switch - covering Layer 2 and Layer 3 features, including key differences in handling unicast IPv4 and multicast traffic.

The session also includes practical troubleshooting strategies and hands-on demonstrations of packet capture tools. You’ll learn tips and best practices for identifying and resolving common network issues, just like a Cisco TAC engineer.

Whether you’re deploying new switches or managing existing infrastructure, this session will give you the tools and knowledge you need to keep your network running smoothly.
